Tariff Information

                

                

                    

                        

                            Caj Tenaga (Energy Charge):
                            RM 0.2703/kWh (standard), RM 0.3703/kWh (>1500 kWh)
                        

                        

                            Caj Kapasiti (Capacity Charge):
                            RM 0.0455/kWh
                        

                        

                            Caj Rangkaian (Network Charge):
                            RM 0.1285/kWh
                        

                        

                            Caj Peruncitan (Retail Service Charge):
                            Waived for <600 kWh, RM 10.00/month for ≥600 kWh
                        

                        

                            Cukai Perkhidmatan (Service Tax):
                            8% applies for usage >600 kWh
                        

                        

                            KWTBB (1.6%):
                            Applies for usage >300 kWh
                        

                        

                            Insentif Cekap Tenaga (EECI):
                            Rebate up to -RM 0.25/kWh for usage <1000 kWh
